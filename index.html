How It Works

  1. Paste your text — any article, post, or caption.
  2. AI analyzes it — a transformer model runs in-browser.
  3. Get hashtags — key topics become hashtags.
  4. Copy & use — click to copy one or all.

FAQ

Is my text sent to a server?

No. Everything runs in your browser via WebAssembly/WebGPU.

Does it work offline?

Yes — the model is cached after your first visit.

What AI model does it use?

A compact Hugging Face transformer, optimized for the browser via Transformers.js.
