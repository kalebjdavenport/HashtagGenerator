FAQ

Is my text sent to a server?

No. All three methods run entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

Does it work offline?

Yes — the model is cached after your first visit.

What AI model does it use?

A compact Hugging Face transformer, optimized for the browser via Transformers.js.
